# UniShare browser extension privacy policy

Updated: 2 November 2026. Applies to Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome.

The extension saves only the page the user selects. When Save tab is confirmed it processes that
page's URL, title and chosen collection. Optional offline saving also processes rendered text and
up to twelve readable images. This content goes only to UniShare on the same computer through
`http://127.0.0.1`. The extension does not transmit library content, pairing keys or browsing history
to a remote UniShare service, Microsoft, Google or advertisers. It has no analytics or advertising.

Connection settings and the pairing key stay in this browser profile's local extension storage.
The browser independently contacts its store for native extension updates; its own privacy policy
governs that traffic. The extension requests only its own installation identity, not an inventory
of installed extensions. No extra management permission is requested.

Users choose every save, can disable offline capture, and can edit or delete saved items in UniShare.
Removing the extension clears its settings but does not delete the UniShare library. The library and
its backups remain under the user's control. Privacy/support questions can be filed at
https://github.com/erzod31/UniShare/issues without including private links or pairing keys.
